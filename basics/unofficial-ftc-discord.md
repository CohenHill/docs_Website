# Unofficial FTC Discord

The Unofficial FTC Discord is a community-driven platform where members of the FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) can connect, share insights, and collaborate. Here’s why you should consider joining:

* **Networking**: Connect with team members, mentors, and FTC alumni from across the globe.
* **Resource Sharing**: Access a wealth of resources, including code snippets, tutorials, and project ideas.
* **Event Updates**: Stay informed about FTC events, competitions, and key dates.
* **Tech Support**: Get advice and troubleshooting tips for your robot designs and programming challenges.
* **Community Engagement**: Engage in lively discussions, share successes, and celebrate accomplishments together.

Joining the Unofficial FTC Discord can enhance your FTC experience through collaboration, learning, and community support.
